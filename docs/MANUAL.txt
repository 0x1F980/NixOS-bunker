man bunker

MODEL
  Zone = VM. apps[] / mem / diskGb on the zone.
  invisible+layer = Shufflecake. panic = keep|lock|wipe.
  Brokers: net usb voice. One TUI: bunker.

COMMANDS
  bunker
  bunker-zone list|add|set|rm|apps|usb|rename
  bunker-zone-start <zone|all>
  bunker-sflc unlock|lock|status <layer>
  bunker-panic
  bunker-term <zone>
  bunker-clip send|copy|clear
  bunker-usb-attach|detach <zone> <vid:pid>
  bunker-killswitch enable|disable|status
  bunker-wipe <zone>

SoT: config/zones.json  then  nixos-rebuild switch --flake .#host
